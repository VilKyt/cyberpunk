## Who She Is

> *For the GM only.*

When the Director sealed the party in the tomb, she was the one who got away. She was the Archivist's student; he went into the machine, she went into hiding. Neither knows the other survived.

She followed the lich across the centuries the way the Club Blood remnants did — not as a predator, but as a witness. She could not break the tomb without the Director feeling it, and she could not face him alone. So she waited, and she guarded the ground, and she told anyone who came near it to stay away.

Over the last century she has lived among Night City's people under short mortal identities, always in Watson, always leaving before anyone noticed she did not age properly. She has been doing small, quiet good — and waiting for a moment that never seemed to come.

Then the nomads cracked the seal early. Before she was ready. Before the Archivist was found. Before she had a plan.

> *"You shouldn't be here yet."*

## What She Knows

- Who the party are. She knew them in Wildemount, by reputation if not in person — your call at the table.
- The Archivist as he was. His name. *She will not say it. It is his to say.*
- Wards — abjuration at a level no one else alive in this world can match. The ink is a toy version of what she can do.
- That the drain is real, how it feeds, and that the Director has been looking for her for a very long time.

**What she does not know:**
- That the Archivist survived, in fragments, in three nodes under Night City.
- What the Tower is for. She has felt the city's pull toward Arasaka Tower and suspects it is a harvest. She does not know it is nearly ready.
- That Mireille's "death" did not fully work. The Director stopped searching the name. He did not stop wondering.

## Reaching Her

The threshold in the dead zone (Quest D) stays shut until she decides otherwise. She is watching through the crow.

**Ways in:**
- **The Artist's dead-drop address.** She can no longer reach it from inside the fold — but a message left there stirs something. The crow comes to the party within a day.
- **Proof of the Archivist.** If the party brings anything carrying his signal — a node connection log, Kaz's rig, the query format — and offers it at the threshold, the ward opens. Not for them. For him.
- **The Tower.** If the party tells her through the threshold where souls go when people die in Night City, she opens the fold. She has suspected it. Hearing it confirmed by clean souls is enough.

**When she opens it:** she is smaller than they expect, and tired. The fold inside is a single room that looks like a Wildemount study, kept exactly as she remembers one. The stone she left them — *"I see you"* — has a twin on her desk.

---

## What She Offers

- **The ink, at scale.** The Artist's formula protects one person at a time from the passive drain. She can teach the full version. It cannot stop the Tower. It can make some people invisible to the harvest when it comes — the one lever the party has against something that "cannot be stopped".
- **Wards for the party.** Real ones. Protection from scanners for crossings (see `world/border-crossings.md`), or from the Director's direct attention, for a time.
- **The reunion.** If the Archivist is restored and she is brought to a node, the recognition scene has a second person in it. Run it quietly.

**What she asks:** that they do not bring the Director to her door. She has kept that door shut for a very long time.

---

## Personality

Precise, dry, older than her face. She speaks Common like someone who learned it from books and Elvish like someone who has not had anyone to speak it to in centuries. She is not cold — she is out of practice. Every kindness she does is quiet and deniable, because loud kindness got people killed.

She is frightened of the Director. She has never let that make a decision for her.
