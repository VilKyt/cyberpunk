## The Premise

A party of D&D adventurers from Wildemount were defeated by a lich
and sealed in a soul-drain tomb. A thousand years later — in 2077 —
nomads crack the seal outside Night City. Magic still works. The lich
is now the hidden will behind Arasaka.

Soulkiller is his distributed phylactery. It has worked once: in 2023,
it pulled Johnny Silverhand out of his dying body and onto a chip.
Fifty-four years of refinement later, the Director is close to switching
it on for the whole city. What he still lacks is a clean soul to
calibrate against — so the harvest *holds* instead of just hollowing.

The party are the cleanest souls on the continent.

They woke up at exactly the wrong moment.
Which might also be the only moment that matters.
