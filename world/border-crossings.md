# BORDER CROSSINGS
*Leaving or entering Night City is never routine for clean souls.*

---

## The Scanners

Every official crossing — city checkpoints, Badlands border posts, the highway gates — has a soul-integrity reader bolted beside the standard ID arch (see Act 1, Scene 4 for the first one). Ghost IDs clear the ID layer. Nothing clears the soul layer except luck, wards or a way around.

## The Luck Roll

Each crossing, each character rolls a **d20** (flat, no modifiers unless something below applies).

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| **11+** | Clean pass. The scanner reads noise and moves on. |
| **2–10** | **Anomaly logged.** The blue light flickers. Nothing happens now. It goes into `CVGNC-OPS` as one more data point — counts toward Arasaka notoriety escalation (`world/notoriety.md`, Tier 1 → 2 on a pattern). |
| **1** | **The scan fails completely.** Not a flag — a blank where a soul should be. The Director is notified directly, and the Checkup Room wakes. |

**Modifiers (optional):**
- Another character disrupting the scanner (netrunner or magic, as in Act 1, Scene 4): advantage for whoever passes in the window.
- Myrael's wards (`npcs/myrael.md`), once she gives them: automatic pass for a number of crossings you decide.
- Arasaka notoriety Tier 3+: a roll of **1–2** counts as a complete failure. The scanners have been told to look for them.

**Avoiding it:** smuggler routes, storm drains, nomad convoys and the like avoid the roll entirely — and bring their own problems.

---

## The Checkup Room

Every control outpost has a small side room marked **CORPORATE COMPLIANCE — MEDICAL VERIFICATION**. Clean, white, one chair, one cabinet. Travellers who get pulled for a "random check" sit here for ten minutes while a medtech asks dull questions.

In the corner, by the door, stands an **Arasaka security officer** in full dress uniform. Helmet on. Perfectly still. Staff call him "the mannequin" and stopped noticing him months ago.

He is not a mannequin. He is a **Vessel** — a body whose soul was drained to nothing, kept alive on a drip and a neural link. There is nobody inside. The Director can step into him at any moment, from anywhere.

**On a complete scan failure:** the Director is notified within seconds. He does not always come — but when he does, the mannequin's head turns.

### What the Director does with the Vessel

Pick one, depending on what he wants from the party at this point:

- **Look.** The Vessel walks out into the checkpoint and simply *looks* at them. Then it speaks — the Director's voice, calm, through a dead man's mouth. This may be the first time the party hears him directly. Then the Vessel goes still again.
- **Hold.** The Vessel locks the outpost down and holds them until a response team arrives (8 minutes, as in Act 1, Scene 4).
- **Stop them.** The Vessel attacks. It is not trying to kill; it is trying to disable the clean souls and keep them there.

Whatever happens, the Vessel's eyes show the deepest purple-black the party has seen — the corruption completely full.

## Stat Block — The Vessel

*Medium humanoid (hollowed), lawful evil while ridden*

**AC** 17 (Arasaka tactical armour) **HP** 110 (13d8 + 52) **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18 (+4) | 14 (+2) | 18 (+4) | 20 (+5)* | 18 (+4)* | 20 (+5)* |

*\*Only while the Director is riding it. Otherwise 1, 1, 1 and the Vessel does nothing.*

**Saving Throws** Con +8, Wis +8 **Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened, stunned
**Senses** truesight 30 ft. (the Director's), passive Perception 14
**Challenge** 7 (2,900 XP)

***Nobody Home.*** The Vessel cannot be persuaded, deceived or intimidated. Psychic damage does nothing — there is no mind there to hurt.

***Puppet String.*** The Director is not in the room. If the Vessel is reduced to 0 hit points, or its neural link (a port at the base of the skull, AC 18, 10 HP, called shot at disadvantage) is destroyed, the body drops. Destroying the link counts as *destroying Soulkiller-adjacent infrastructure* (+2 Arasaka notoriety).

***Recognition.*** The Director learns everything the Vessel sees: faces, abilities, which character cast what. Next time he will be prepared.

**Actions**
- ***Multiattack.*** Two strikes, or one strike and one spell.
- ***Strike.*** *Melee:* +8 to hit, 2d8 + 4 bludgeoning. The target makes a DC 16 Con save or is grappled — the grip does not loosen on its own.
- ***Smart Pistol.*** *Ranged:* +6 to hit, 80/240 ft., 2d6 + 2 piercing. Ignores half cover.
- ***Borrowed Magic (3/day).*** The Director casts through the Vessel (save DC 17): *hold person*, *counterspell*, *dispel magic*, *slow*.

**Reactions**
- ***Unfinished.*** When the Vessel would drop to 0 HP for the first time, the Director can let it go — or spend this reaction to keep it standing with 1 HP and speak one sentence before it falls.

> *GM note: the Vessel is not a boss fight. It is the Director tapping on the glass. Use it to put a face and a voice on him before the Tower, and to make every crossing feel like a dice roll with consequences.*
