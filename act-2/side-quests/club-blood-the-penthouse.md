# CLUB BLOOD — THE PENTHOUSE
*Act 2. Follows [Club Blood](quest-club-blood.md) when the party helps Tobias Rieper with the contract (Option A).*

---

## GM Background

> *For the GM only.*

47's target is **Teodor Vasharr** — one of Club Blood's ancient members and the one who actually carries out the Director's removals. The client's daughter was one of his. Cover identity: **Adrian Kessel**, private art collector, owner of the top three floors of the **Corvane Residence**, a glass tower in Charter Hill (Westbrook).

He is old enough to know magic exists and careful enough to plan for people who know it too. His lair has corporate security for thieves, wards for anyone smarter, and a sealed mausoleum for the hours when he is helpless.

**Day or night changes everything:**
- **Day:** he sleeps in his coffin inside the warded mausoleum. The spawn sleep. The building is quiet. The smart glass is blacked out. Getting to him is a heist.
- **Night:** he is awake, there may be a party on the dining floor, and the fight is on his terms.

47 has done his homework. He recommends day. He will support either.

## The Building

**Corvane Residence**, 63 floors. The lower 60 are luxury apartments with a private security contractor (not Arasaka — the arrangement makes sure of that). Floors 61–63 are Kessel's, reached by a private elevator keyed to his biometrics, or by the fire stairs (sealed doors, alarmed).

| Floor | What's there |
|---|---|
| **61 — The Salon** | Dining hall and party space. Glass walls on three sides, long table, bar, a sunken lounge. Where Club Blood's upper tier entertains the living. |
| **62 — The Rooms** | Bedrooms. No windows — interior floor, soundproofed. Sealed sleeping pods for the spawn; guest suites for the "blood dolls" who stay the night; Kessel's own bedroom (which he does not sleep in). |
| **63 — The Gallery** | His collection: art, relics, valuables, a vault. Glass walls again. At the centre, under a skylight that has been bricked over from above, the **mausoleum**. |

### Physical Security

- **Private elevator:** biometric (Kessel's hand and retina). A netrunner can spoof it (DC 16) or the party can climb the shaft.
- **Fire stairs:** steel doors, contact alarms. Opening one alerts the building contractor — **6 minutes** to a four-person response team. They are ordinary guards. They do not know what lives upstairs.
- **Cameras and motion sensors** on every floor, run from a local controller on 63 (behind the bar's back panel on 61 there's a junction for it).
- **Two ceiling turrets** in the Gallery. Smart pistols, +6 to hit, 2d6 piercing, fire at anything without Kessel's tag.
- **The glass:** armoured smart glass on 61 and 63. Polarises to full black at sunrise, with steel blackout shutters behind it. Both run from the **shutter relay** on the roof (see *The Sun*).

### Magical Security

- ***Alarm*** on the stair doors and elevator of 63 — mental, silent. **He wakes.**
- ***Glyphs of warding*** on the vault and the three best display cases (DC 16 Investigation to spot; Arcana or *dispel magic* to disarm). Triggered: *hold person* (DC 16) or 5d8 fire in a 20-ft sphere.
- ***The Sepulchre Ward*** — see below.

## The Mausoleum and the Sepulchre Ward

A small marble tomb built inside the Gallery, the size of a garden shed. Wildemount style — old Elvish funerary script on the lintel. Inside: a coffin lined with earth he brought from home, a thousand years ago.

Around it: a dome of shimmering force. It works like *wall of force* — nothing passes, nothing damages it, except *disintegrate*. Kessel passes through it freely.

**The ward is anchored to three stones**, hidden around his floors. *Detect magic* shows all three as faint red points. Each is set inside something:

1. **Floor 61** — the crystal heart of the dining hall chandelier.
2. **Floor 62** — under the floor of his bedroom, beneath the bed he never uses.
3. **Floor 63** — inside the frame of the most valuable painting in the collection. Breaking it out ruins the painting (35,000 eddies, gone).

**Rules:**
- An anchor has AC 15, 20 HP, or can be pried out (Strength DC 15, or thieves' tools DC 15).
- **Every anchor broken makes Kessel stir** — roll a d20. On 10 or lower, he wakes.
- **All three broken:** the ward falls.
- **Two broken:** the ward flickers. *Dispel magic* at DC 13 drops it.
- **None broken:** *dispel magic* won't touch it; *disintegrate* will.

The looters in the party will want floor 63's painting. That is the point.

## Teodor Vasharr — "Adrian Kessel"

Looks forty. Grey eyes, perfect suit, the voice of someone who has never had to raise it. He is courteous right up to the moment he isn't. He knew the Director before the transformation, and he has done his errands for a very long time without ever once feeling bad about it.

### The Charm — Dominion

He doesn't make people like him. He makes people **want what he wants.**

Under Dominion the victim's mind is calm, even happy. He can tell someone to walk into the rooftop pool and stay under — and they will, gladly, while their body thrashes and fights to come up. The mind isn't resisting. Only the body is.

This is the scene to build the fight around: a friend, smiling, drowning, and the rest of the party having to choose between saving them and stopping him.

### Stat Block

*Use the Vampire (MM p.297) as the base. Changes below.*

**AC** 16 **HP** 144 **Speed** 30 ft., climb 30 ft.
**Challenge** 13

> *Party level 5–7? Run him at 110 HP and Dominion DC 15. The real win conditions are the coffin, the sun and 47 — not trading blows.*

**Replace Charm with:**

***Dominion.*** Kessel targets one creature he can see within 30 ft. — **or any creature on floors 61–63 that can hear him through a speaker, intercom, earpiece or cyberaudio implant.** Wisdom save DC 17. A creature that succeeds is immune for 24 hours.

On a failure the creature is **dominated** until Kessel dies, is more than a mile away, or it breaks free:
- It believes Kessel's commands are its own wishes. It is content.
- He can order it to harm itself, including fatally. It obeys.
- **Taking damage does not grant a new save.** The mind does not fight.
- **The body does.** At the end of each turn in which the creature is hurting itself or suffocating, it makes a **DC 15 Constitution save**. On a success it struggles for that turn — it does nothing useful but takes no further harm from its own actions. It is still dominated.

**Breaking free** (the dominated creature gets a new Wisdom save, with advantage where noted):
- An ally uses an action to physically drag it out of danger or pin it (contested Athletics).
- Someone it loves speaks to it — a real memory, a name, something only they would know. Persuasion DC 15 → re-save with advantage.
- Kessel takes 30+ damage from a single hit (47's rifle, usually) → every dominated creature re-saves.
- *Calm emotions*, *protection from evil and good*, *dispel magic* (DC 17), *greater restoration*.

He can hold **three** creatures this way at once.

**Spells (cyberpunked flavour, same mechanics):**
- ***Smart House (lair only).*** In place of Children of the Night: the building's systems answer him. Locks seal, turrets retarget, lights die, a pool cover slides shut over someone. Once per day, in the lair, he can also summon 2 vampire spawn from floor 62's pods (they arrive at the end of the next round).
- ***Mist in the Vents.*** Misty Escape works through the HVAC. At 0 HP outside his coffin he dissolves into the vents and is back in the coffin in 2 hours — unless the ward is down and the coffin is destroyed or staked shut.

**Legendary Actions (3/round):** as Vampire, plus:
- ***Whisper.*** One dominated creature immediately takes an action Kessel chooses (uses its reaction).

**Lair Actions (initiative 20, only if he is awake in the penthouse):**
1. **Blackout.** Every light on one floor dies. Darkvision only.
2. **The House Speaks.** He uses Dominion on any creature on the three floors that can hear a speaker or an implant — no line of sight needed.
3. **Lockdown.** Every door on one floor locks (thieves' tools or Strength DC 17).

**Weaknesses (as Vampire — tell the players what 47 already learned):**
- **Sunlight:** 20 radiant damage at the start of his turn, disadvantage on attacks and checks.
- **Running water, stake through the heart** (while in the coffin, incapacitated), **forbiddance**: as MM.
- **Radiant damage** stops his regeneration.
- *Campaign rule:* **fire** also stops his regeneration for one turn. 47 heard "fire" in the exchange and came prepared.

## The Rest of the Household

**Floor 62, pods — 4 vampire spawn** (MM p.298). Unlike the tunnel spawn these are still *people*: Club Blood members, sane, dressed for an evening. They sleep through the day. **Gunfire or a broken anchor:** roll a d6 per round; on a 1–2, one spawn wakes and comes looking.

**Night only — 2 bodyguards** (Veteran, MM p.350, with reflex boosters: +2 initiative, one extra reaction per round). Human. Already dominated for years. They will die for him and think it's their own idea.

**Night only — guests on floor 61.** Twenty of the city's rich, some of them blood dolls. Mostly harmless. Every one of them is a hostage he can Dominate.

## 47 — Overwatch

He is in a maintenance level of the tower across the street, 400 metres out. Prone, still, patient. He will not enter the penthouse under any circumstances.

**He communicates by text only.** He has turned his earpiece off. (*"You described the charm. I would rather not hear his voice."*) Players who keep an open voice channel in the lair are giving Kessel a way into their heads.

**Line of sight:** floors 61 and 63 through the glass. Not 62. When the blackout shutters are down, he is blind.

**His shot:**
- Acts on **initiative 15**, or as a reaction when a player calls a target by text.
- **+12 to hit, 4d10 + 8 piercing** (~30). Crits on 19–20.
- The first shot through a pane of armoured glass does half damage and shatters it. After that it's open air.
- **2 incendiary rounds** (fire — stops Kessel's regeneration for a turn).
- **1 shot reserved for the shutter relay** if the party asks for it.

**What 47 can't fix:** dominated party members. He will not shoot one. He will text: *"Your friend is in the water."*

## The Sun

If the fight happens in daylight and Kessel wakes, the **shutter relay** on the roof is the most important object in the building.

- 47 can destroy it with his reserved shot (one turn's warning by text).
- A netrunner can open the shutters from the building controller on 63 (DC 16, or DC 13 from the junction on 61).
- The relay itself is on the roof — physically reachable from the fire stairs.

When the shutters open: the smart glass loses power and goes clear. Floors 61 and 63 fill with daylight. Kessel takes sun damage every turn he stays on those floors. He will retreat to floor 62, which has no windows — or to the mausoleum, if the ward is still up.

## After

- **47** confirms the kill by scope, texts *"Contract complete,"* and meets them the next night as agreed. He delivers the **Arasaka Tower schematics** (lower and mid levels) and states the terms of his one further service. This is the party's first real way into the Tower.
- **Ilvara** (`locations/club-blood.md`) knows within a day that Teodor is gone. She does not grieve him. She wants to know who managed it, and she will ask the party to her table — curious, careful and no longer indifferent.
- **The Director** loses his removal service. He notices. Whether he connects it to the party depends on how clean it was — a recovered ledger with Arasaka routing in it is a reason for him to look.
- **The client** gets her answer. The party can decide whether she ever learns what it was.
